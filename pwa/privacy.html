Privacy & Legal

Version 1.0 · 2026-11-14 · Applies to the offline, self-hosted FieldSeal Suite.

1. Plain-language summary

English. FieldSeal Suite runs entirely on your device. It has no backend, no account, no telemetry, no analytics SDK, no cookies and no outbound network calls. Evidence, cases, signatures and the local operation trail stay in your browser profile or in files you export yourself. The publisher never receives your data. You are the controller of any personal data you choose to enter; the publisher is not a processor because it has no access.

3. Your rights (GDPR Art. 15–22)

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection are exercised locally by you, because no external controller holds a copy:

  • Access / portability: open Backup & restore and export all cases plus the JSON/TXT operation trail.
  • Erasure: delete a case from Case Management; the linked operation-trail entries are removed and surviving actor references are redacted automatically. Clearing browser site data removes everything else.
  • Rectification / restriction: edit draft cases directly; sealed cases are intentionally immutable, so rectification means creating a corrected new case and deleting the old one.

Rectification, restriction and objection (GDPR Art. 16 / 18 / 21). Draft cases can be edited in place. Sealed cases are intentionally immutable, so rectification means creating a corrected new case and deleting the old one; restriction is applied by keeping the original sealed and adding a linked note; objection to a processing purpose is exercised by not entering the relevant data and deleting the case.

You may also send a written request using the template below. The publisher has no access to your data and is therefore not a recipient of such requests; the template is provided for your own records. We aim to respond within one month (extendable by two months for complex requests, with reasons given).

5. Legal disclaimers

This software produces authenticity and chain-of-custody artefacts. It does not provide an eIDAS qualified electronic signature, does not make legal determinations, and does not guarantee admissibility in any court. Admissibility depends on local law, procedure and the operator's own testimony.
